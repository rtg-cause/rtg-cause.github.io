About the Meeting

Exchange. Discuss. Connect.

The Joint Meeting brings together doctoral researchers and senior researchers from German Research Training Groups for scientific exchange, discussion and networking.

The annual meeting of German Research Training Groups provides an opportunity for young researchers to present their work, exchange ideas across research areas and establish new scientific connections.

The 2027 meeting will take place at Schloss Dagstuhl – Leibniz Center for Informatics from 23 to 26 May 2027.

The meeting is organised by the Research Training Group CAUSE.
