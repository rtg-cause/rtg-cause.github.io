The University of Bremen’s online magazine _up2date_ has published an article about the CAUSE Research Training Group. Featuring the work of our doctoral researchers Nicola Thoben and Caroline Dominik, the article showcases the collaboration between researchers at the universities of Bremen and Oldenburg.

Read the full article: [Developing Explainable Systems Together](https://up2date.uni-bremen.de/en/article/developing-explainable-systems-together)
