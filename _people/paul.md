I am researching the explainability of optimisation algorithms, particularly stochastic, population-based metaheuristics. I aim to make these algorithms more transparent for both end users and optimization experts, and to demonstrate how various parameters influence performance and why certain algorithms and configurations are suitable for specific problems.
